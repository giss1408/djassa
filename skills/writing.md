# Skill: Writing

Purpose: produce clear, concise, and audience-appropriate text in French (primary) and English (secondary) for `djassa`.

When to use:
- Drafting report sections, executive summaries, and outreach content.

Style rules:
- Use plain French, avoid jargon where possible.
- Keep paragraphs short (3–5 sentences) and include one key idea per paragraph.
- Provide a short summary (2–4 sentences) at the top of each section.

Prompt pattern:
- Task: "Draft a 300–500 word section on <topic> in French with a 3-sentence executive summary and three key bullets."

Contributor notes:
- Mark uncertain claims with [citation needed] and link to `skills/research.md` for sourcing.
