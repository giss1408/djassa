# Skill: Research

Purpose: conduct structured, evidence-based research on financial inclusion topics relevant to `djassa`.

When to use:
- Preparing literature reviews or background sections.
- Sourcing statistics, reports, and primary data on inclusion financière.

Recommended outputs:
- Short annotated bibliography (title, author, year, 2-sentence summary, relevance).
- Data source list with license and access notes.

Prompt pattern:
- Task: "Find authoritative sources on <topic> (since <year>), summarize key findings, and list datasets with access links and licenses."

Contributor notes:
- Prefer official sources (World Bank, IMF, national statistics, peer-reviewed journals).
- Record search queries and date retrieved.
